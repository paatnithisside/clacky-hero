Colour

An ivory and pastel palette. Each accent has one job: sky marks selection and the playhead, sage marks success, peach marks a highlight, lilac is the cursor ripple. The darker “ink” step of each hue is only for thin lines and tiny marks.

Motion

One procedure, three steps, about 4.7 seconds, then rest. Each step finishes before the next begins and lights one of the headline’s full stops. The resting styles are the final frame, so reduced motion and no-JS visitors see the finished composition.

Primary button

A matte keycap, since Clacky started as a click-sound app. On hover the key lifts and its dark edge grows with it, so more of the side shows as it rises. Pressing it sinks the key.

.btn.btn--primary

Quiet button

The secondary action: a 1.5px outline and no fill, so it never outranks the keycap. Hover adds a faint ink wash; pressing nudges it down 1px.

.btn.btn--quiet

Small nav button

The primary button at nav size. It uses the same rise-and-edge mechanism, scaled down: 4px of lift and a 6px edge on hover. Below 360px its label shortens to “Try free”.

.btn.btn--primary.btn--sm

Studio

The hero object: a window that turns a raw screen recording into a captioned demo. A caption appears, the transcript line is selected, a filler word is struck out, the cut shows on the timeline, and the status chip flips to “Ready to share”. Click inside it for the product’s own ripple.

.win.studio

Analytics

A small card that tucks over Studio’s lower corner. A headline number with a sparkline, the top two sources as bars (the winner picks up the peach highlight), and two summary figures. It is labelled “Sample data”.

.win.an

Task completed

A macOS-style notification that lands last, on top of Studio’s edge. It has no reserved space: it overlaps the other windows on purpose.

.notif

Cursor ripple

The product’s click ripple, in lilac. On the left it is shown at rest under the cursor; on the right it follows your click. Click inside the box. The same effect runs when you click inside the Studio window.

.ripple .cursor .pulse

Replay button

A pill with a circular arrow that reruns the three-step procedure. It sits under Studio, is hidden for visitors who prefer reduced motion, and grows to 44px on touch screens.

.replay

Trust band

Complementary text only: four short facts between two hairlines at the very end of the hero, with three thin dividers. It is deliberately quiet and may fall below the fold. Wide screens show one row; narrower ones a 2×2 grid; phones a single column.
